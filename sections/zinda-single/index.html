Calculation rules

  • Net KG = Total KG − Jaal KG. Basic Amount = Net KG × Rate / KG.
  • Samiti = 1% of Basic Amount, rounded down to the whole rupee.
  • Labour by average bird size (Net KG ÷ Pieces): below 2.50 kg → Pieces ÷ 9 × ₹5 · 2.50–2.99 kg → ÷ 8 × ₹5 · 3.00 kg and above → ÷ 7 × ₹5.
  • Labour is adjusted upward (max ₹9.99) so the bill lands on a multiple of ₹10. A labour amount you type replaces the auto labour.
  • Balance Baaki = Pichla Balance + Aaj ka Bill − Rec Cash − Rec Online.
  • Number boxes accept sums like 50+60.5+94.
